# Test a four-day workweek

## Intended result

Increase completed deep-work sessions by at least 25 percent while maintaining
team responsiveness and Thursday evening energy.

## Constraints and assumptions

The main prediction is that removing Friday meetings will create uninterrupted
work capacity earlier in the week. Maya assumes unfinished administrative work
will not simply expand into Thursday evening.

## Actions

1. Schedule focused work before noon Monday through Thursday.
2. Move routine coordination into two fixed windows.
3. Keep Friday free of routine work for six weeks.
4. Record completed deep-work sessions, urgent Friday interruptions, and
   Thursday evening energy.

## Success signals

- At least 25 percent more completed deep-work sessions.
- No material decline in team response time.
- Thursday evening remains available for shared plans at least five of six
  weeks.

## Review

Final review completed 2026-07-01. Deep-work output barely changed and Thursday
evening energy worsened, so the trial ended. See the
[outcome](../outcomes/four-day-workweek-trial.md).
