# Run a four-day-workweek trial

## Question

Should Maya permanently change her schedule or test it first?

## Context

Friday currently produces little focused work. A permanent change would affect
teammates and shared plans before Maya knows whether the extra day improves
anything.

## Alternatives considered

- Keep the current schedule and change nothing.
- Permanently compress work into four longer days.
- Run a six-week trial with explicit success signals and a final review.

## Decision

Run the six-week trial.

## Rationale

The trial creates evidence while keeping the schedule reversible.

## Consequences

Maya will protect Friday from routine work, track deep-work sessions and energy,
and review the result before extending the arrangement.

## Reconsider when

Team coverage suffers, Thursday work regularly expands into the evening, or
the trial shows no meaningful benefit.
